Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains what information SprintVision (“we”, “us”) collects through the SprintVision app (the “App”) and our website, sprintvision.app, how we use it, and the choices you have.

The short version

  • Your sprint videos are analysed on your phone. Nothing is sent anywhere to be analysed.
  • When you save an analysis, the clip and its results are stored in private cloud storage linked to your account, so you can open it on any device.
  • If you join a coach’s team, your coach can see the analyses and sessions in that team. Teammates can’t see each other’s data.
  • We don’t sell your data, and we don’t use it for advertising.
  • You can delete your analyses, or your whole account, in the App at any time.

Who we are

SprintVision is responsible for (the “controller” of) the personal data described in this policy. You can contact us at contactsprintvision@gmail.com.

Information we collect

Your account

  • Your email address, and your name if you add one or it comes from Google or Apple
  • An account identifier used to sign you in

You can sign in with your email address (using a password or a one-time code we email you), with Google, or with Apple. If you use Sign in with Apple and choose to hide your email, Apple gives us a private relay address instead.

Athlete profiles

A profile holds an athlete’s name and height. Height is used to turn the analysis into real distances, such as step length and speed. Coaches can create profiles for the athletes they manage.

Your videos and analyses

  • Analysed on your phone. When you choose a video, the App tracks body position in each frame and measures every step on your device. Your video isn’t uploaded to be analysed.
  • Saved analyses. When you save an analysis, we store the trimmed clip, the tracked body positions and camera movement, a thumbnail, the name and details you give it, and its results in private cloud storage linked to your account. A copy is also kept on your device. This lets you reopen your analyses on any device, including after reinstalling the App.
  • Each account has a storage limit, shown in the App’s settings.

Calendar and sessions

  • Events that you or your coach add to your calendar, such as training sessions, gym, races and physio, including their exercises, notes, links and any video clips attached
  • Which sessions you complete, and any rating and note you add about how a session felt

If you add a link to a session, the App fetches a preview (such as the title and image) directly from that website.

Setup questions

When you first open the App, we ask a few questions: whether you’re an athlete or a coach, your event, the part of the race you most want to improve, what has been holding you back, how you review your runs, how many athletes you coach, and your personal best. We also record how far you get through setup and whether you start a subscription. We use this only to understand who uses SprintVision and to improve the App.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, we store your device’s push notification token so we can let you know when a session is added, changed or removed, and send session reminders. Notifications you receive are also kept in the App’s inbox. Some reminders, such as the end of a free trial, are scheduled on your device only.

Purchases

Subscriptions are bought through the Apple App Store or Google Play and managed with RevenueCat. We receive your subscription status and purchase details, linked to your account, but never your card details. Custom plans for larger squads may be billed through Stripe, which processes the billing details you give it; we receive only what we need to set up and manage the plan.

Diagnostics

If the App hits an unexpected error, it sends us a short report so we can fix it: the error message and technical details, the App version, your device’s operating system, and your account identifier and email address. Reports never include your videos, analyses, passwords or payment details.

Our website

If you use our contact form, we receive your email address, subject and message, and use them to reply to you. The website loads its fonts from Google Fonts, so Google receives your IP address when you visit. The website doesn’t use advertising or tracking cookies.

Permissions

The App asks for these permissions only when it needs them, and you can change them at any time in your device settings:

  • Photos: to choose the videos you analyse, and to save videos and reports you export
  • Camera: if you choose to film a sprint in the App
  • Notifications: for session updates and reminders

How we use your information

  • To create your account and sign you in
  • To analyse your sprints and show your results
  • To store your saved analyses, calendar and sessions, and keep them in sync across your devices
  • To share team content between you and your coach
  • To send the notifications you’ve allowed
  • To manage subscriptions and access to paid features
  • To find and fix errors, and to keep the App secure
  • To understand who uses SprintVision and improve the App
  • To reply when you contact us

We don’t use your data for advertising, and we don’t sell it.

Our legal bases

If you’re in the European Economic Area or the UK, we rely on these legal bases:

  • Contract: most of the processing above is needed to provide the App you signed up for, including your account, analyses, calendar, team features and subscription.
  • Legitimate interests: diagnostics, security, and understanding how the App is used (including the setup questions), in ways you would reasonably expect.
  • Consent: notifications, and access to your photos and camera. You can withdraw consent at any time in your device settings.
  • Legal obligation: where the law requires us to keep or disclose information.

Coaches and teams

Coach and Club plans let a coach manage a team of athletes.

  • What your coach can see. Your coach can see your athlete profile, the analyses saved within the team, the sessions they assign to you, which sessions you complete and any ratings and notes you add, and how much storage your team content uses. Teammates can’t see each other’s data.
  • Joining a team. Your coach invites you with a private link or code. Before you join, you’re shown what your coach will be able to see and asked to confirm you’re 16 or older. Analyses you saved outside the team stay private to you.
  • Team access. While your coach’s plan is active, you can use paid features as part of the team. If the plan ends, or you leave or are removed from the team, that access ends and you lose access to the team’s content. You keep your own analyses and can start your own subscription.
  • Profiles managed by a coach. A coach can create a profile and save analyses for an athlete who doesn’t have their own account. The coach is responsible for having permission to do so, including a parent’s or guardian’s consent for an athlete under 16.

Who we share it with

We share information only with the providers that run the App for us, with your coach when you’re on their team, or when the law requires it:

  • Supabase: sign-in, database, private file storage and server functions
  • Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications and App Store purchases
  • Google: Google Sign-In and Google Play purchases, and Google Fonts on our website
  • RevenueCat: subscription management
  • Stripe: billing for custom plans
  • Resend: delivering diagnostic reports and contact-form messages to our inbox
  • GitHub: hosting our website

Body tracking runs on your device using Google’s MediaPipe software. Your video frames aren’t sent to Google. Each provider processes data under its own terms and privacy policy.

International transfers

Some of our providers process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. Where they do, the transfer is protected by safeguards such as the European Commission’s Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

How long we keep it

  • Saved analyses: until you delete them or your account
  • Calendar, sessions and feedback: until they’re deleted or you delete your account. Sessions a coach created stay with their team.
  • Account and setup answers: until you delete your account
  • Diagnostic reports: only as long as we need them to fix the problem
  • Contact messages: as long as we need them to deal with your message

Files you export, for example to your photo library, stay where you saved them, so delete them there if you no longer want them.

Deleting your data

  • You can delete any saved analysis from your history in the App.
  • You can delete your account in Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your account and the data stored with it, including your saved analyses and clips.
  • Deleting your account doesn’t cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play settings.

Your rights

You can ask us to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, or to restrict or object to how we use it. You can also withdraw any consent you’ve given. Email contactsprintvision@gmail.com and we’ll reply within one month. If you’re in the European Economic Area or the UK, you can also complain to your data protection authority. In Ireland, that’s the Data Protection Commission (dataprotection.ie).

Security

Your data is encrypted in transit, and your saved analyses are kept in private storage that only you, and your coach for team content, can access. No system is completely secure, and copies on your phone are also protected by your device’s own security.

Children

The App isn’t intended for children under 13. You must be 16 or older to create your own account. A coach can manage an athlete under 16 as a profile, without a login of their own; the coach is responsible for getting consent from the athlete’s parent or guardian.

Earlier versions of the App

Versions of the App before 1.2 also offered AI coaching reports and training plans. To create them, the numeric results of an analysis (never the video) and, if you had added them, your height, event and personal bests were sent through our server to Google’s Gemini AI. Those versions also shared anonymized sprint metrics, without your name, email or video, to help us benchmark performance. Version 1.2 and later do neither. AI reports you created earlier stay with those saved analyses until you delete them.

Changes to this policy

We’ll post any changes on this page and update the date at the top. If a change is significant, we’ll also let you know in the App.

Contact

Questions about this policy or your data: contactsprintvision@gmail.com
