What to fix

Know exactly what to work on next.

Your set position, drive phase and arm action are rated against targets from sprint research, with a cue for anything outside the range.

Set: front knee

108°OK

Target 85–105° · 3° straighter than the target

Lower the hips slightly, or bring the front block a little closer to the line.

Torso lean, first steps

54°OK

Target 35–50° · 4° more lean than the target

Cue: “push the ground away”, not “fall forward”.

Arm swing

146°Good

Target 120° or more · In the target range

Big arm swing that balances the legs.

Set: front hip

52°Good

Target 35–52° · In the target range

In the typical range for a powerful first push.

Filming

Just press record.

Film side-on, follow the run down the track, or film from behind. SprintVision keeps tracking you as the camera moves, so you can film however suits your session.

  • On the straight. Film runs on the straight, not around the bend.
  • Head to feet. Keep the whole athlete in shot for the whole run.
  • Not too far away. The athlete should fill a good part of the screen.
  • One athlete. Keep other people out of the shot.
  • Real-time video. Film at normal speed so every step is timed accurately.
  • Want a split? Keep the start and finish in shot, then mark them on the video.
