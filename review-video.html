Download video

On iPhone/iPad, tapping Download saves the file so you can add it to Photos.

  1. Tap “Download video” above. Safari saves it to the Downloads folder.
  2. Open the Downloads menu (the arrow icon in Safari's address bar) and tap the file to open it in Files.
  3. In Files, press and hold the video, choose Share, then Save Video — it's now in Photos.
  4. In SprintVision, start a new analysis and pick this video from your Photos library.
