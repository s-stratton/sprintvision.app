# Blog keyword research

Last done: 31 October 2026. Not published (Jekyll skips `_blog/`).

## Method and limits

- **Google and YouTube autocomplete** (`suggestqueries.google.com`, US English): 20 seed terms each expanded with a–z and "how to / best / for", plus 28 more seeds. That's about 620 queries and 3,600 unique phrases.
- **Score** = sum over every list a phrase appeared in of (10 − its position). Phrases that come up often and near the top score higher. This is a **relative popularity signal, not search volume**. Seeds that were expanded more can score higher just from that.
- **Who ranks**: checked the top results for the main candidate topics.
- **Still needed**: real monthly volumes. Paste the phrases below into Google Keyword Planner (free with a Google Ads account), and after launch, use Search Console to see which queries actually bring visitors.

## What ranks now

- **Broad terms** ("how to sprint faster", "sprint technique", "sprinting form"): Nike, MasterClass, IMG Academy and Red Bull, plus a lot of scraped spam. Hard to win, so they're covered by the hub post (proper-sprinting-form) and the long-tail posts link back to it.
- **Stride frequency / length, ground contact time**: mostly research PDFs, SimpliFaster and forums. A clear, well-structured explainer can rank here.
- **S-MAS**: almost only academic papers and physio summaries. No practical athlete guide, so this is the easiest win.
- **Sprint start without blocks**: PDFs, Scribd and old forums. Weak results.
- **"sprint video analysis app"**: other apps' App Store pages. High intent; the homepage and app listing should target it.
